# UNDERSTUDY — Market Research

**Date:** 30 July 2026
**Method:** desk research, MECE-decomposed across four independent axes
**Prompted by:** the honest answer to "was sufficient research done?" — **no.** The prior work
validated *supply of transitions* and never checked whether the product already exists.

**Reading key:** ▸ sourced fact · ◇ our inference · ⚠ contradicts something we previously asserted

---

## 0. Executive summary

1. **Our exact square is empty. Every square adjacent to it is occupied and better funded.**
   Nobody sells knowledge capture at the *moment of SMB ownership transfer*. The published
   advice for that moment is "use a shared drive and record some videos."
2. ⚠ **The capture mechanic is already commoditized — in B2C.** *Tell Mel* runs an AI biographer
   that phones an elderly person on an ordinary phone call, no app, no login, no typing. *Remento*
   already ships a QR code that plays the original voice. Our "22-minute call" and "hear Bud say
   it" are **not novel and not defensible.**
3. ⚠ **The lender channel thesis is factually wrong.** SBA business-acquisition loans have the
   *lowest* default rate of any 7(a) purpose — 0.71%, roughly 3× lower than new-business loans —
   and median time to default is **4.1 years (49.3 months)**, not months 6–18. The persona claim
   we built a customer proposal on does not survive contact with data. Correction in §6.
4. **Japan is the proof market and the hardest market.** 2.45M owners over 70 by 2025 with about
   half lacking a successor; 53.9% successor-absent rate; a national public support network
   handling 24,000+ new consultations a year; BATONZ went public in 2026.
5. **Korea is the strategically interesting market,** for a reason that is not demographics:
   there is a national push to convert veteran tacit knowledge into AI training data, and the
   government's own framing admits that **IP rights and fair compensation for the knowledge
   contributor are unresolved.** That is a policy-shaped hole that fits our consent, provenance,
   and owner-payment design exactly.
6. ◇ **Therefore the moat cannot be capture. It has to be elicitation targeting and the
   consequence model.** Detailed in the strategy meeting: [MEETING-MINUTES.md](MEETING-MINUTES.md).

| **A2** | Matching & brokerage | **KR:** KMX (10,000+ deals), 마톡, 딜플러스, M&A거래소 (9,660 listings / 4,000 active buyers), 리스팅, 중기부 M&A 정보망 · **JP:** BATONZ (#1 by users/deals/closings 2021–2025, IPO 2026), TRANBI, 日本M&Aセンター · **US:** BizBuySell and peers | **Crowded in every geography. This is what we killed at M2 — correctly.** |
| **A3** | Financing | SBA 7(a) lenders (US), 정책자금 (KR) | Not a product square for us |
| **A4** | **Transition / knowledge handover** | **Nobody, as a product.** Published guidance is checklists: "set up a shared file system or playbook, use video recordings, meeting transcripts and checklists" | **Empty. Our square.** |
| **A5** | Post-transition operations | FSM/ERP (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro); Teamshares' bundled software | Occupied; we sit beside it, not against it |
| **A6** | Alternative endings | ESOP / employee ownership (Teamshares, Project Equity); KR: 폐업 지원 — 976k closures in 2025, 238k by owners 60+ | Adjacent; Teamshares is the one to watch |

**The A4 evidence, stated plainly:** the ETA literature describes our problem precisely —
*"buyers often inherit operational complexity without structured support or knowledge transfer…
sellers typically disengage quickly, taking critical know-how with them"* and *"seller value
decays rapidly after month three."* It then recommends manual practice. **A well-documented pain
with no product against it is the single best signal in this research.**

◇ Counter-reading we must hold: a well-documented pain with no product sometimes means the pain
is real but *nobody will pay to fix it at that moment*. §7 kill criteria address this.

## 2. MECE Axis B — How the knowledge actually moves today

Exhaustive over substitute mechanisms, from zero-technology to enterprise AI.

| # | Mechanism | Representative players | Price anchor | Threat to us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | Do nothing / informal shadowing | — | $0 | **Highest.** This is the real competitor |
| **B2** | Human services (integration advisors, 승계 컨설팅) | Regional consultants, exit planners | High, unscalable | Medium — the trap we could become |
| **B3** | SOP & documentation tools | Trainual, Scribe, Tango, SweetProcess, Notion | $30–300/mo | Low — they need someone to already know what to write |
| **B4** | Frontline / connected-worker platforms | **Poka** (video tribal-knowledge capture before technicians retire), **Dozuki** (CreatorPro AI: narrated video → interactive work instructions), Augmentir, Tulip, Parsable, Librestream | **$25,000–$100,000+/yr** mid-size | **High.** Enterprise-priced, well-funded, one segment above us |
| **B5** | AI tacit-knowledge capture | **US:** eGain, Tacit, Glitter AI, Superkind, iFactory · **JP:** 技能継承くん (Airion, Univ. of Tokyo spinout — draws out tacit knowledge through AI dialogue), 匠AI (Mitsubishi Research Institute), NTT Data × Lion · **KR:** national initiative to convert 숙련공 tacit knowledge into AI training data | Enterprise | **Highest strategic threat.** The category exists and is 2026-active |
| **B6** | Consumer AI biographers | **Tell Mel** (AI biographer, scheduled phone calls, *"no apps, no logins, no typing"*), Remento (voice + QR to original audio; Shark Tank), StoryWorth (1M+ families), HereAfter AI · **KR:** 엄마의 인터뷰 | $50–300 one-off | ⚠ **Commoditizes our mechanic** |
| **B7** | Public programs | **JP:** 事業承継・引継ぎ支援センター — 24,000+ new consultations FY2025, 92,509 sessions, 2,265 M&A closings · **KR:** 중기부, 소진공, 백년가게 · **US:** SBDC | Public | Channel, not competitor |

**The B5/B6 squeeze is the central competitive fact of this research.** Above us, well-funded
enterprise vendors capture tacit knowledge for *employers about employees*. Below us, consumer
apps capture life stories for *families about parents*. Both use our mechanic. Neither serves a
buyer who has 54 days of contractual access to a departing owner and $672K of revenue riding on
what that person remembers.

◇ **The gap between B5 and B6 is not a market nobody wants. It is a market whose customer only
exists for 90 days,** which is exactly why neither a seat-based enterprise vendor nor a
consumer subscription app has gone after it.

## 5. What is genuinely novel, and what is not

| Component | Novel? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| AI phones an elderly person, no app or typing | ❌ **No** | Tell Mel ships this in B2C today |
| Playing the original voice at the point of use | ❌ **No** | Remento's QR-to-audio |
| Turning tacit knowledge into structured explicit knowledge with AI | ❌ **No** | 技能継承くん, 匠AI, NTT Data, eGain, Poka, Dozuki CreatorPro |
| Documenting SOPs for a small business | ❌ **No** | Trainual, Scribe, Tango |
| **Elicitation targeted by consequence** — asking the next question that protects the most revenue | ✅ **Yes** | No player found ranks questions by financial exposure |
| **Coverage weighted by revenue exposure** rather than document count | ✅ **Yes** | Enterprise vendors report completion; nobody reports exposure |
| **The 90-day ownership-transfer trigger** as the product's occasion | ✅ **Yes** | A4 is empty in all three geographies |
| **Consent + provenance + contributor compensation as product architecture** | ✅ **Partly** | KR policy explicitly flags this as unsolved |

◇ **Conclusion: we are not a capture company. We are a targeting company that happens to
capture.** If we describe ourselves by the mechanic, we are commodity. If we describe ourselves
by the ranking, we are alone.

## 6. ⚠ Correction to a previously stated claim

**Previously asserted** (persona statement, carried into the customer proposal): *"My defaults
cluster at months 6 through 18 — after the seller leaves and before the buyer has learned the
business."*

**What the data says:** SBA business-acquisition loans have the lowest default rate of any 7(a)
purpose at **0.71%**, roughly 3× lower than new-business loans; **median time to default is 4.1
years (49.3 months)**; the peak danger zone is years 2–5. Historical portfolio loss at 92 months
is ~0.9%.

**Consequence:** the lender channel had one argument — "we reduce your charge-offs" — and that
argument is not available. A lender operating at 0.71% has no charge-off crisis for us to solve.

**Actions taken:**
- The customer use proposal was re-pointed at the buyer. The lender case is demoted to an
  appendix that states the 0.71% figure against our own interest.
- The lender-first channel recommendation is **withdrawn**.
- ◇ Note this independently supports the instruction to build technical barriers before pursuing
  partners. Had we run the pilot first, we would have spent months learning this in a meeting room.

## 7. What this research cannot tell us

Stated so nobody mistakes desk research for validation:

1. **Whether a 66-year-old owner will consent.** No published consent-rate data exists for this
   population. Unknowable without contacting owners — which the current constraints forbid.
2. **Whether the buyer pays $14,500 at closing.** No comparable price point exists at A4.
   B4's $25K–$100K/yr enterprise anchor is suggestive, not evidence.
3. **Whether targeted elicitation actually beats generic interviewing.** This one is testable
   without any external party, and it is the verification protocol in the meeting minutes.
4. **Whether Korea's policy gap becomes a program we could serve.** Requires relationships we
   are not permitted to assume.
